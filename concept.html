Flower Lab

On the table

The box, the probes, the cable, the server.

All of it sits in one place. The cabinet tends the plant. A cable crosses the table to a switch, and another cable ends at the server that keeps the record.

Light

A bar of diodes keeps the day. If the bay gets warm, it backs off.

Drink

Three bottles, one pipe, a ring on the soil. Near outlets get more of the feed. Dry gaps remain.

Probes

A three-prong probe and a flat blade. Their cables are clipped to the wall and end at the computer.

Record

The computer decides. The switch only joins the cable. The server at the end of the table keeps what was sent.
