Flower Lab

Plan

One cabinet. The drawing is the object. The notes around it are what it does. Readings go to the main server.

1

Light

The hood holds the hours and brightness you set. If the bay gets too warm, the light backs off.

2

Water

A probe in the soil asks for a drink. The cabinet gives a small one, then stops if the soil is wet, the tank is low, or the day is capped.

3

NPK

A three-prong probe in the pot reads nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium.

4

Feed

When a reading falls short, the cabinet doses A or B. It does not feed when the soil is already in range.

5

Report

An Orange Pi Zero 3 sits in the dry side. It posts every reading and every dose to the main server. The server collects them and keeps the aggregate.
