Flower Lab

Concept

One pot · one cabinet

A tabletop cabinet for one plant.

It keeps the light, waters when the soil is dry, and reads nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium in the pot.

Goals

Three jobs.

Light

Keep the day.

You set hours and brightness. The hood holds that schedule, and backs off if the bay gets too warm.

Water

A small drink.

You set how moist the soil should stay. The cabinet waters, then stops if the soil is wet, the tank is low, or the day’s cap is hit.

Nutrients

Read the soil.

The NPK probe reports nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium. When a reading falls short, the cabinet doses A or B. It does not feed when the soil is already in range.
