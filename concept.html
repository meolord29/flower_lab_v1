On the table

One plant, in a box on the table.

The hood keeps the day. The soil asks for water. Feed is added only when the pot is short, and only into water that is already on its way up.

Light

The hood keeps the day you set. If the bay gets warm, the light backs off.

Water

A dry pot gets a small drink. The cabinet waits, checks, and stops before it floods.

Feed

It reads the soil. A and B go up only when a reading is short, carried in the water.

Record

What it saw and what it did is sent on. The box still tends the plant if that send fails.
