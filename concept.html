Flower Lab

Product

A cabinet for one plant.

It keeps the light, waters when the soil is dry, and doses A or B only when nitrogen, phosphorus, or potassium is short. An Orange Pi Zero 3 decides. The main server only collects.

Jobs

Four things it is for.

Light

You set hours and brightness. Channel 0 of the PCA9685 dims the 12 V bar. If the bay is too warm, the duty falls, including to off.

Water

A capacitive probe on the ADS1115 asks for a drink. The water pump runs for a few seconds, then the cabinet waits and reads again. It stops if the soil is wet, the float says the tank is empty, or the day's cap is hit.

Nutrients

The RS485 probe reads nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium a few times a day. Short on nitrogen, pulse A. Short on phosphorus or potassium, pulse B. In range, pulse neither. A and B join the water line. They never run alone.

Report

Every reading and every dose is posted. The server stores them and keeps hourly and daily aggregates. If the POST fails, the cabinet still acts and retries with the same id.

Control

The Pi decides. It does not power anything.

Pumps and the LED sit on their own 12 V supply, switched by a MOSFET board. Ground is common. 12 V never touches a pin. On boot, all four channels are off.

Setpoints live on the Pi: on and off times, light duty, moisture band, pulse lengths, NPK lows, and the hours between nutrient doses. The server cannot open a MOSFET.

Records

What leaves the cabinet.

Readings

moisture, temperature, npk, light, float. Vision is added later. NPK is omitted on cycles that did not read it.

Each row has an id, a cabinet id, and recorded_at from the Pi. The server adds received_at.

Doses and faults

A dose is a pulse or a light change: channel, seconds or duty, and a reason such as moisture_low, n_short, too_warm, float_empty, or daily_cap.

A skipped pulse is not a dose. A fault names the source: ADC, NPK, PWM board, float, temperature, or, later, the camera.

Aggregates are computed on the server, by hour and by day: moisture average, temperature range, light-on time, water and concentrate seconds, and the last NPK sample. The cabinet does not send them.

API

One POST. No remote pump.

CallWhoWhat
POST /v1/cabinets/{id}/batchCabinetReadings, doses, and faults in one body. 202 counts new rows and duplicates.
GET /v1/cabinets/{id}/aggregatesA personHour or day buckets. The control loop never calls this.

Retries

The id is chosen on the Pi and reused if the call times out. A repeated id is the same row, not a second dose. A 400 is dropped. A 401, 404, or 5xx stays in the queue.

Not in this version

No endpoint turns a pump on. No setpoint is written from the server. Later vision posts a label and a confidence, not the frame.

Later

A camera for the leaves, not for dosing.

A CSI camera on the Zero 3, because USB is the RS485 adapter. A small YOLO model runs on the CPU, a frame every few minutes. The board has no NPU. It tracks leaf yellowing, browning, spots, and bloom stage, and those events are posted. The water and nutrient loops do not read it.

Longer notes, including the setpoint list and the error table, are in docs.
