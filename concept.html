On the table

One pipe up. A ring on the soil.

Water and feed share the tube that climbs the wall. At the pot that tube becomes a ring, so the drink lands around the plant, on the soil, not on the leaves and not in one hole.

Light

The hood keeps the day you set. If the bay gets warm, the light backs off.

Water

A dry pot gets a slow ring of drops on the soil. The cabinet waits, checks, and stops before it floods.

Feed

A and B join that same pipe, only when a reading is short. The near outlets get more of that dose. Water follows and flushes the rest.

Record

The computer collects and decides. The cable on the table carries that record to the switch.
